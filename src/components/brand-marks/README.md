# Brand marks

**The only place in the app allowed to hold a hex value.**

`AGENTS.md` forbids colour at a call site because the app's chrome may not belong to any one
house. A third-party sign-in mark is the opposite case: Google's four colours and Apple's
silhouette are that company's property, specified by their brand guidelines, and re-tinting them
to a gray step would be both wrong and against those guidelines.

So the rule holds with its reason intact — colour that belongs to someone else lives as data, in
its own folder, and never leaks into a token. The same principle carries a card brand's accent in
`Brand.accent`.
